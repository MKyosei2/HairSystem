# Brick look and parting tuning

This stage specifically strengthens the two items that were still weak:

1. Procedural scalp region / parting masks are stronger and more off-center.
2. Brick chain rendering is pushed further away from tube-like shading and proportions.

## Changes
- Narrower, more forceful procedural part line
- Stronger crown / temple / nape region shaping
- Stronger asymmetric guide distribution
- Smaller cross section and shorter segment depth for brick rendering
- Slight per-brick width jitter and more faceted lighting
